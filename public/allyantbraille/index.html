Our mission

Who we are

To improve the quality of life and provide inspiration and opportunity for people who are blind or have low vision through employment, outreach, rehabilitation, education, and research.

Every order creates opportunity

Envision is a nonprofit social enterprise and one of the nation's largest employers of people who are blind or have low vision. Employment is the heart of our mission.

Career pathways span manufacturing, services, and professional roles, supported by job readiness and assistive technology training that help people build lasting careers. When you order braille from Envision, you support that work directly.
